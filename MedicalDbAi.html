Hospital Data Management & AI Diagnostics System

A comprehensive web application designed for managing hospital electronic health records. The platform enables multi-role management for doctors and system administrators, allowing efficient entry of patient data, automated statistical correlation analysis, and clinical decision support through AI-assisted potential diagnoses and treatment recommendations.

Project Details & Technologies:

Project Type: Master's Dissertation Thesis (In Progress)
Backend & Database: PHP, MySQL
Artificial Intelligence: Predictive Analytics & AI Clinical Decision Support
Frontend & Security: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Role-Based Access Control (Admin / Doctor)

View Repository

Key Features

Multi-Role Authentication

Secure access control featuring dedicated dashboards for Administrators (system management & users) and Doctors (patient records & clinical workflows).

Data Analytics & Correlations

Automated generation of medical statistics and data correlations based on patient records introduced into the system.

AI Diagnosis & Treatment

AI-assisted clinical decision module analyzing symptoms and medical histories to suggest potential diagnoses and personalized treatment schemes.
